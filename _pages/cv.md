## Appointments and experience

- **Postdoctoral Scholar, UC Berkeley** (2025–present): quantum and classical algorithms for partial differential equations and high-dimensional dynamics.
- **Graduate Student Researcher, UC Berkeley** (2019–2025): continuum models and numerical methods for curved, deforming lipid membranes.
- **Chemical Engineering Research Intern, NOVA Chemicals** (2017–2018): process modelling, data analysis, and engineering diagnostics for polymer manufacturing.

My current work includes continuum mechanics and membrane dynamics, quantum algorithms for scientific computing, finite-element methods, scientific software, mathematical visualization, and verification-oriented workflows for coding agents.
